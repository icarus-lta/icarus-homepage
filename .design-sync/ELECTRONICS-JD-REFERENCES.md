## Editorial choices

- Eight responsibility bullets covering system architecture, device communication,
  MCU firmware, optional custom boards, batteries/BMS, power and harnesses,
  troubleshooting/test iteration, and interface documentation.
- Core qualifications emphasize firmware, at least one named communication
  interface, circuits, datasheets, bench measurements, and hands-on project work.
- PCB design, deeper battery/power experience, RTOS, autopilot integration,
  EMI mitigation, and test automation are preferred experience.
- Board tools are examples, not declarations of ICARUS's current toolchain.
- BMS duties focus on selection, integration, monitoring, and verification; custom
  battery-cell manufacture or a from-scratch BMS is not assumed.
- Flight-control algorithm development is not assigned to this electronics role.
- No minimum years, degree requirement, high-voltage target, aerospace certification,
  export-control condition, or foreign compensation/benefit is copied.
- Korean copy retains noun-ending bullets; company introduction remains prose.
- Salary remains determined after interview. No stock options, offer-letter stage,
  recruiter details, or Notion link is added to the posting.
