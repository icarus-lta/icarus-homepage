## Copy decisions

| Page section | Basis | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Our beginning | Two questions inspired by Peter Thiel; belief that airships need renewed innovation | Short English paraphrases, not presented as verbatim quotes |
| The search | Review of earlier high-altitude projects and discussions with researchers/practitioners | Focus on the company's investigation; do not assert definitive causes of other projects' endings |
| Our roots are in control | Explicit origin in drone-control research; airship flight/ground software; experimental model checks | Describe the foundation and work performed without superlatives or performance figures |
| Beyond the controller | Envelope-material development and collaboration with 한국섬유개발연구원 | Explain how the work extends into materials; no patent ownership or quantitative comparison claims |
| Our mindset | Challenge, learning, courage, commitment; design/fabrication feedback | Proposed editorial wording derived from the recruitment and workplace notes |

The notes establish no reliable dated ICARUS milestone sequence, so numbered chapters describe the story rather than inventing a dated timeline. Mission details remain on the main page. Employment terms, salaries, profile details, world-first/world-best claims, and unverified historical dates are not repeated here.
